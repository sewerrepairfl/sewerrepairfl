## Common Sewer Problems

Homeowners may experience several warning signs of sewer line trouble, including:

- Multiple drains becoming slow or clogged.
- Toilets bubbling or making unusual gurgling sounds.
- Sewage backing up into showers or bathtubs.
- Persistent unpleasant sewer odors.
- Repeated drain blockages.
- Unusually wet or sunken areas in the yard.

These symptoms can have different causes. A professional assessment can help determine whether the issue involves a local drain, the main sewer line, or the public sewer system.
